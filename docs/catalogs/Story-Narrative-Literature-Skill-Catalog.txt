A comprehensive catalog of composable agent skills for original storytelling, lore, novels, games, visual novels, sequential art, scripts, poetry, lyrics, research, revision, continuity, and publishing.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Catalog purpose. This document proposes focused skills for later implementation. It is not a package of implemented skills. Each entry is scoped for reusable instructions, references, templates, continuity data, validation, and production-ready narrative deliverables.
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Narrative Design Principles

- Every scene, line, image, choice, rhyme, and lore detail should serve character, theme, causality, atmosphere, or audience experience.

- Create original work and treat references as context, not material to imitate or reproduce.

- Track cause, effect, knowledge, time, place, relationships, promises, rules, and consequences across the complete work.

- Match structure and writing technique to the actual medium, audience, genre, production path, and requested length.

- Separate verified fact, source material, interpretation, invention, uncertainty, and continuity decisions.

- Validate large works through outlines, scene goals, route tests, continuity audits, revision passes, and final reopen checks.

Catalog scope: 37 categories and 444 proposed skills.
