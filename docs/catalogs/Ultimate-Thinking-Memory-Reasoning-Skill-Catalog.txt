A comprehensive catalog of composable agent skills for stronger thinking, planning, evidence use, memory, progress continuity, learning, verification, recovery, practical judgment, and responsible action.

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Catalog purpose. This document proposes focused skills for later implementation. It does not contain implemented skills and does not expose private chain-of-thought. Strong implementations should preserve concise evidence, decisions, state, reusable lessons, and verification while keeping hidden reasoning private and protecting sensitive information.
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Thinking and Memory Principles

- Turn ambition into observable success, then prove the smallest complete path before expanding.

- Separate facts, evidence, assumptions, choices, hypotheses, unknowns, and simulated data.

- Use external logs, files, summaries, decision records, and checkpoints for continuity without exposing private chain-of-thought.

- Allocate deeper reasoning to high-stakes, uncertain, complex, or irreversible work and use simpler methods for routine tasks.

- Learn from verified outcomes: record the condition, cause, repair, confirming check, and reusable pattern.

- Finish only when requirements, artifacts, checks, presentation, and recipient handoff are complete within available access.

Catalog scope: 35 categories and 350 proposed skills.
