How to Use This Catalog

Each item is a candidate Skill that can later become a focused Copilot package with a SKILL.md entrypoint, optional scripts, references, assets, evaluation cases, and safety checks.

The catalog is intentionally broad. A production catalog should combine closely related items when one workflow and evaluation suite can cover them reliably, and split items when they require different tools, permissions, models, or risk controls.

Descriptions define the intended capability and quality bar. They do not claim that any specific model, dataset, connector, or license is already available.

How Modern Diffusion Image Generation Works

1. Training on Very Large Image-Text Collections

- Learning patterns: During training, an image model may process extremely large collections of images paired with captions or related text. Training adjusts numerical parameters so the model becomes better at predicting visual structure associated with language. The exact dataset size and composition vary by model.

- Numbers, not pictures: Computers represent an image as arrays of numerical values. A common RGB image has height, width, and three color channels. Training pipelines normalize these values, transform them into tensors, and learn statistical relationships among edges, shapes, materials, lighting, composition, and words.

- Latent space: Many diffusion systems compress images into a lower-dimensional latent representation using a variational autoencoder. The model learns to operate in this compact mathematical space, where related visual features occupy structured regions. Latent space is not a literal gallery of stored pictures.

- Training objective: A diffusion model is shown progressively noised examples and learns to predict the noise, velocity, flow, or clean representation needed to reverse the corruption. Optimization updates billions of parameters over many batches.

2. Translating Text into Conditioning Data

- The prompt is tokenized into words or word fragments. A text encoder converts those tokens into vectors called embeddings.

- Cross-attention connects text features with spatial image features. This allows terms such as 'cat,' 'space suit,' 'silver,' or 'moonlight' to influence different regions and attributes.

- Prompt order, token limits, weighting, negative conditioning, and the capabilities of the selected text encoder affect how faithfully instructions are followed.

3. Sculpting an Image from Noise

- Starting with static: Generation commonly begins from seeded random noise in latent space. The seed makes the initial noise reproducible when the rest of the settings remain the same.

- Step-by-step denoising: A scheduler chooses noise levels across a sequence of timesteps. At each step, the denoiser predicts how the latent should change to become more consistent with the prompt and any control signals.

- Guidance: Classifier-free guidance and other conditioning methods balance prompt adherence against natural variation. Excessive guidance can create harsh contrast, oversaturation, or artifacts.

- Decoding: After the final latent is produced, the VAE decodes it into pixels. Optional refiners, face repair, inpainting, and upscaling improve selected aspects.

4. Beyond Basic Diffusion

- Image-to-image starts from an encoded source image and adds a controlled amount of noise before denoising. Lower denoising strength preserves more of the source; higher strength allows larger change.

- Inpainting uses a mask to regenerate selected regions. Outpainting expands the canvas and synthesizes new context beyond the original border.

- ControlNet, pose, depth, edges, segmentation, and reference adapters provide stronger spatial or identity guidance than text alone.

- Fine-tuning methods such as LoRA, DreamBooth, and textual inversion specialize a base model, but require lawful data, careful evaluation, and safeguards against memorization or overfitting.

Recommended Blueprint for Turning Catalog Items into Real Skills

Trigger definition

State exactly when the Skill should activate, what requests are out of scope, and which competing Skills take priority.

Inputs

Define accepted prompts, reference images, masks, model files, workflow JSON, dimensions, formats, brand guides, and policy constraints.

Outputs

Specify image formats, editable sources, metadata, provenance, comparison grids, reports, and naming conventions.

Tool routing

Name required generation engines, editors, command-line tools, APIs, connectors, GPU environments, and fallback routes.

Workflow

Encode the shortest reliable sequence: inspect inputs, choose model, generate a small test, evaluate, refine, export, and verify.

Evaluation

Include normal, boundary, failure, safety, reproducibility, and visual-regression cases with measurable pass criteria.

Governance

Record rights, consent, privacy, retention, disclosure, provenance, and human approval requirements.

Recovery

Preserve the last passing output, isolate the failing stage, vary one factor, rerun affected checks, and keep exact settings.
