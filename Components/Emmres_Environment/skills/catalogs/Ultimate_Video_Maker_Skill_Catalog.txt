How to Use This Catalog

This catalog is a blueprint for a powerful video-making agent or a coordinated family of specialist agents. It names capabilities and expected outcomes. It does not implement the skills.

Combine focused skills through an orchestrator for complete productions. A typical workflow can accept raw clips, images, PNGs, logos, documents, music, sound effects, voice recordings, and instructions, then produce editable projects, review previews, and final masters.

Every implemented skill should preserve source links, identify assumptions, expose controls, produce editable outputs when practical, and validate normal, boundary, and recovery cases.

Implementation Priority Blueprint

A practical build order that reduces rework and creates useful end-to-end capability early:

Phase 1: Reliable Foundation
Ingest, organization, scripts, shot planning, basic editing, audio sync, captions, exports, versioning, and quality checks.

Phase 2: Professional Finishing
Motion graphics, transitions, color, dialogue repair, music, SFX, compositing, reframing, mastering, and platform versions.

Phase 3: Advanced Creation
Animation, VFX, tracking, keying, restoration, generative video, specialized genres, and high-complexity effects.

Phase 4: Scale and Safety
Automation, collaboration, rights, privacy, accessibility, localization, regression checks, delivery manifests, and archival.

Phase 5: Autonomous Production
Multimodal interpretation, selects, rough cuts, capability routing, repair agents, adaptive compute, approvals, and full orchestration.
