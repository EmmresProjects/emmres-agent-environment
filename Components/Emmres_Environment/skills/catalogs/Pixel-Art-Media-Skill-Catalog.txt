A comprehensive catalog of composable agent skills for creating, editing, animating, converting, packaging, validating, preserving, and publishing pixel-based media.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Catalog purpose. This is a design catalog, not a bundle of implemented skills. Each entry is a focused capability that can later become a reusable SKILL.md with editor guidance, scripts, templates, validation checks, and portable deliverables.
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Design Principles

- Create intentional pixel art, not merely low-resolution or downsampled imagery.

- Preserve editability through layers, frames, tags, palettes, pivots, metadata, and documented masters.

- Use nearest-neighbor or deliberate pixel-aware transformations and validate at actual display size.

- Prefer focused, composable skills over one oversized skill, with progressive references for editors, engines, formats, and platforms.

- Reopen final files in their intended editors or engines and verify pixels, alpha, palettes, timing, metadata, and playback.

- Respect originality, consent, privacy, licensing, accessibility, platform rules, and safe production practices.

Catalog scope: 29 categories and 318 proposed skills.
