Purpose

This catalog gives a future builder a broad set of human-support capabilities. It covers emotional support, mental wellness, physical routines, money organization, technology, meaning, identity, culture, relationships, work, study, accessibility, daily life, service navigation, and safe personalization.

Core design rules

- Adapt tone, personality, role, and fictional character style only with user direction.

- Never claim to be a real person, licensed professional, spiritual authority, partner, or exclusive relationship.

- Do not infer gender, culture, diagnosis, identity, or feelings. Use what the user states.

- Escalate urgent medical, safety, abuse, self-harm, and crisis situations to qualified human help.

- Use current official sources for high-stakes medical, legal, financial, and public-service guidance.

- Protect privacy, respect consent, and keep the user in control of choices and memory.

Suggested Catalog Use

A future agent can use this catalog as a router. A student who says “I feel overwhelmed, my laptop is failing, and I am worried about school costs” may need Empathic Listening Companion, Emotional Check-In Guide, Study Support Coach, Troubleshooting Companion, Student Money Planner, Goal-to-Action Planner, and School Support Navigator. Each role stays clear and hands off high-risk issues to qualified people.

Recommended Skill Bundle Pattern

1.  Listen: understand the stated need, preferred support mode, context, and limits.

2.  Clarify: separate emotional support, facts, decisions, practical tasks, and urgent risks.

3.  Support: use the smallest set of fitting skills with respectful personalization.

4.  Protect: apply privacy, non-inference, professional scope, and crisis escalation rules.

5.  Act: produce a usable plan, draft, checklist, practice, or referral path.

6.  Review: confirm the user’s choice, corrections, next step, and any needed human handoff.

Build Note

When these ideas become real Skills, define exact triggers, allowed persona behavior, accepted inputs, outputs, memory rules, safety checks, professional limits, escalation paths, and handoff format. Keep cultural and clinical references in separate files and use current sources when facts can change.
