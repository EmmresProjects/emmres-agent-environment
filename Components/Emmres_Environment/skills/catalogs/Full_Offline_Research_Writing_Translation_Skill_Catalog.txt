FULL OFFLINE RESEARCH,
WRITING, AND TRANSLATION
Comprehensive Skill Catalog

A capability blueprint for air-gapped corpora, scholarly retrieval, legal and standards research, OCR, speech transcription, multilingual translation, evidence synthesis, writing, citation, factuality, rights, provenance, and human review.

Catalog edition: November 2026

Purpose and Scope

This document is a planning catalog for future reusable skills. It does not contain the corpora or implement the listed skills. Each entry is scoped so a stronger agent can convert it into a standalone skill or coordinated module. The target environment is fully offline or air-gapped, with licensed local sources, versioned indexes and models, citation-level provenance, multilingual benchmarks, explicit copyright controls, and human review.

Design Principles for Future Skill Builders

- Preserve source identity, edition, date, jurisdiction, language, page or timestamp anchors, licenses, and complete provenance.

- Distinguish primary, secondary, tertiary, corrected, retracted, superseded, and unverified sources before synthesis.

- Ground factual writing in retrievable evidence, attach citations to claims, surface conflicts, and abstain when evidence is insufficient.

- Pin corpus snapshots, indexes, embedding models, rerankers, language models, OCR, ASR, translation models, prompts, and terminology resources.

- Apply copyright, quotation, redistribution, privacy, and restricted-access rules before ingest, indexing, generation, export, or packaging.

- Evaluate retrieval, factuality, translation, terminology, OCR, transcription, writing, and citation quality by language and domain.

- Package the stack so another offline workstation can install, search, verify, translate, write, review, reproduce, and continue the work.

Recommended Catalog-to-Skill Package

When converting an entry into an executable skill, keep the control file compact and place repeatable detail in supporting resources:

- SKILL.md: precise trigger description, workflow, source rules, permissions, uncertainty, failure conditions, validation, and completion behavior.

- agents/ui_metadata.yaml: display name, short description, icon, and brand color.

- scripts/: deterministic offline ingest, indexing, search, extraction, citation, translation, evaluation, packaging, and recovery tools.

- references/: corpus schemas, source hierarchies, citation styles, terminology, language guides, legal and copyright rules, benchmark rubrics, and troubleshooting.

- assets/: permitted templates, dictionaries, test questions, evaluation texts, citation fixtures, translation samples, and expected outputs.

Final Acceptance Standard

A future implementation should be considered complete only when it installs and runs without network access, searches licensed local corpora, preserves source provenance and freshness, produces grounded citations, respects copyright and redistribution permissions, meets language-specific accuracy thresholds, records uncertainty, passes human review where required, and can be reproduced from a clean offline package.
