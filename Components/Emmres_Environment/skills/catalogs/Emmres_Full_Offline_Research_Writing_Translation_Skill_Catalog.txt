Catalog Purpose

This catalog defines reusable capabilities for full offline research, writing, and translation. It covers authoritative corpora, ingestion, search, evidence evaluation, citation, fact checking, synthesis, drafting, editing, multilingual production, human review, rights, security, reproducibility, and publication. It is a catalog only and does not claim that the listed skills are already implemented.

Operating Principles

- Normal work remains fully offline and uses only approved local models, corpora, tools, and language assets.

- Every material claim, quotation, translation decision, and generated artifact remains traceable to source and version.

- Automation assists retrieval, analysis, drafting, and translation, but high-risk decisions retain explicit human review.

- Copyright, licenses, privacy, quotation limits, and redistribution permissions are enforced at corpus and output level.

- Quality is measured separately by language, domain, source type, and task, then retested from a clean state.

Appendix: Recommended Executable Skill Contract

When a catalog entry becomes an executable skill, define a stable offline contract so it can be evaluated, repaired, governed, and combined with other capabilities.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Field                               Definition
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Purpose                             Exact research, writing, or translation outcome and its non-goals.

  Triggers                            Requests, files, corpus updates, schedules, reviews, or publication states.

  Inputs                              Formats, languages, domains, classifications, versions, and required context.

  Outputs                             Artifacts, structured records, citations, state changes, and uncertainty.

  Local Dependencies                  Models, corpora, indexes, language assets, runtimes, fonts, and parsers.

  Permissions and Rights              Access scope, licenses, quotation limits, redistribution, privacy, and export controls.

  Procedure                           Ordered steps, checkpoints, approvals, retries, and stop conditions.

  Validation                          Source, claim, citation, language, structure, accessibility, and rendering checks.

  Human Review                        Reviewer roles, escalation criteria, adjudication, and final authority.

  Evidence and Provenance             Source coordinates, versions, models, transformations, decisions, and approvals.

  Failure and Recovery                Checkpoint, rollback, corpus repair, reprocessing, and human handoff.

  Benchmarks                          Normal, boundary, multilingual, adversarial, freshness, and clean-state tests.

  Versioning                          Compatibility, migrations, deprecation, corrections, and rollback rules.
