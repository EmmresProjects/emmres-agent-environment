CONSOLE AGENT SKILL CATALOG

Preservation, Homebrew, Emulation, Hardware, Official Development, Testing, and Archival Research

Coverage: earliest dedicated consoles through PlayStation 5 Pro, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, handhelds, arcade systems, FPGA platforms, and PC handheld ecosystems

Purpose

A comprehensive implementation catalog of reusable agent skills for lawful game-console preservation, homebrew development, source builds, authorized debugging, media inspection, compatibility testing, archival research, FPGA development, official platform work, and packaging. It describes capabilities to build and does not claim that every tool, SDK, device, firmware image, or licensed worker is currently available.

Safety and legal boundary

- Use only games, firmware, keys, certificates, accounts, devices, and development materials the operator lawfully owns or is authorized to use.

- Do not bundle copyrighted ROMs, BIOS files, firmware, encryption keys, title keys, console certificates, leaked SDKs, stolen signing keys, DRM bypasses, account bypasses, anti-cheat bypasses, or unauthorized online-service emulators.

- Prefer official SDKs, developer programs, licensed hardware, and platform support channels for modern consoles.

- Verify provenance, hashes, region, revision, hardware, reader, drive, settings, and dump quality for preservation work.

- Emulation availability does not prove title compatibility, legal redistribution, or replacement of official certification.

Host model

Linux x86_64 core with licensed Windows and macOS workers, Linux GPU workers, official developer-program workers, physical consoles, testkits, devkits, media readers, capture equipment, logic analyzers, and FPGA hardware where required.
